# Client intake checklist (before quoting)

Ask these in the first real conversation. They decide the price, the timeline, and whether we can deliver what they want.

## The restaurant
- [ ] Legal business name and address (for the agreement and Stripe).
- [ ] Number of locations. Opening hours.
- [ ] Current website (if any) and who controls it.
- [ ] Which delivery apps they use today and roughly how much they sell on them per month (for the savings report).

## Decision and money
- [ ] Who makes the decision? Who pays? (Get both names. Southern Jerks taught us these can differ.)
- [ ] Budget, and are they OK with 50% deposit per batch?
- [ ] Which batch first (usually website + pickup ordering)?

## Accounts and access
- [ ] **POS:** which one (Square, Clover, Toast, Lightspeed, Shift4, other, none)? Run `pos-readiness-checklist.md` before promising anything.
- [ ] **Who has admin rights on the POS?** Who can create API credentials or approve apps?
- [ ] **Domain:** who owns it, in whose registrar account, and who can change DNS?
- [ ] **Stripe:** will they open one in the business's name (they must; we never hold their money)?
- [ ] Delivery wanted? Their own Uber Direct / DoorDash Drive account, and who pays the courier fee (customer, restaurant, or split)?
- [ ] Loyalty texts wanted? They must agree to the consent rules (opt-in, STOP, quiet hours 8am–9:30pm local).

## Support
- [ ] **Who answers support** at the restaurant (name, phone/WhatsApp)? Who gets the new-order alerts (email + Telegram + phone push)?
- [ ] Do they want the care plan, or pay-as-you-go?
- [ ] Time zone (for alerts, quiet hours and our support window).

## Content
- [ ] Menu with prices (photo, PDF, or link).
- [ ] Logo and photos (or permission to use their Instagram photos).
- [ ] Brand colours / a site they like.
